# Flow Matching with In-Context Priors for Out-of-Distribution Brain Dynamics

**Date:** 2026-06-10  
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11833 (cross-list cs.LG / q-bio.NC)

## Summary
Introduces a generative modeling approach using flow matching with in-context priors to model and predict brain dynamics, particularly in out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios. Code and pretrained models released.

## Key Contributions
- Addresses the challenge of generalizing neural population models to novel stimuli, tasks, or subjects.
- In-context priors allow the model to adapt rapidly using a few examples from the target distribution.
- Improves robustness for real-world neurotech applications (BCI, closed-loop stimulation).

## Current Trends
This reflects the shift toward **generative and foundation models in neuroscience** — moving beyond static representational alignment to dynamic, predictive modeling of brain activity. Pairs well with papers on predictive representations shaped by exploratory experience. Strong connection to the trend of using ML to uncover low-dimensional structure in high-dimensional neural data.

**Implications:** Enables better cross-subject and cross-task decoding, critical for clinical translation of neurotech.
