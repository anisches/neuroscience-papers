## Recent Additions — July 25, 2026

- **Opposite and complementary roles of the two calcium thresholds for inducing LTP and LTD in models of striatal projection neurons** (2026) — Models metaplasticity in LTP and LTD calcium thresholds in striatal projection neurons, showing that threshold sliding acts as a self-limiting synaptic lock for stable memory storage. [details](computation/2025.11.17.688856-calcium-thresholds-striatal-LTP-LTD/summary.md)
- **Expanding the Phenotypic and Functional Evidence for KCNK3 as a Neurodevelopmental Disorder Gene: A New Chinese Case and Drosophila Validation** (2026) — First Drosophila-based functional validation of KCNK3 as a dosage-sensitive regulator of neurodevelopment. [details](invertebrates/sun-kcnk3-drosophila-ndd/summary.md)
- **Temporal Topology Provides an Interpretable Framework for Neuronal Morphogenesis** (2026) — Introduces the Temporal Topological Morphology Descriptor (TTMD) combining persistent homology with time-lapse imaging for neuronal architecture quantification. [details](computation/rigaux-temporal-topology-morphogenesis/summary.md)
- **Altered Cortical Morphological Brain Networks and Their Diagnostic Classification Utility in Major Depressive Disorder** (2026) — Analyzes individual-level cortical morphological networks from 2,719 structural MRIs across four features, with SVM classifiers achieving moderate-to-good diagnostic accuracy. [details](anatomy/sun-mdd-cortical-morphological-networks/summary.md)
- **Pharmacophore-driven design and biological evaluation of heterocyclic PDE4B inhibitors for Alzheimer's disease** (2026) — Designed and synthesised six pyridine-based PDE4B inhibitors via pharmacophore-guided virtual screening with in vivo efficacy in a Drosophila AD model. [details](2026/pujar-pharmacophore-pde4b-2026/summary.md)
- **Structural and Functional Brain Alterations Induced by Noise Exposure: A Comprehensive Review** (2026) — A narrative review synthesizing evidence on noise-induced structural and functional brain changes. [details](2026/venegas-mora-noise-exposure/summary.md)
- **Spectral theory for population density dynamics of spiking neurons with refractoriness** (2026) — Operator-theoretic framework for neuronal population dynamics with refractory time; identifies exceptional points, derives exact transfer function. [details](computation/2607.20699-spectral-theory-refractoriness/summary.md)
- **SACS: A Reproducible, Configuration-Driven Software Framework for Schematic Multi-Region Circuit Simulation and Integrated Analytics** (2026) — Configuration-driven neuroinformatics framework (Python `brain_sim`) for reproducible multi-region circuit simulation with integrated analytics. [details](computation/beyene-sacs-framework/summary.md)
- **Genetic background and sex moderate the effects of adolescent nicotine exposure on adult functional neural circuits and behavior** (2026) — Strain- and sex-dependent changes in adult mouse brain connectivity from adolescent nicotine exposure. [details](2026/unslal-adolescent-nicotine/summary.md)
- **Error Amplification Limits ANN-to-SNN Conversion in Continuous Control** (2026) — Identifies error amplification as the key failure mode in ANN-to-SNN conversion for continuous control; proposes CRPI mechanism. [details](computation/2601.21778-error-amplification-ann-snn-conversion/summary.md)
- **Rat mediodorsal thalamic subdivisions differentially modulate the sensory and affective components of pain through distinct prefrontal pathways** (2026) — MDmc/MDl subdivisions control sensory vs affective pain via differential PV/SOM interneuron targeting in ACC/PrL. [details](anatomy/iben-daoudi-md-pain/summary.md)
- **Large-scale functional coupling and computational modelling reveal frontotemporal hotspots in distributed network connectivity during working memory** (2026) — iEEG from 1,652 recording sites reveals frontotemporal hubs coordinate distributed working memory networks. [details](anatomy/zhang-frontotemporal-hotspots-2026/summary.md)
- **Amyloid-β-Related Cortical Susceptibility Alterations in Individuals Under Assessment for Alzheimer's Disease: A χ-Separation Study** (2026) — χ-separation reveals Aβ burden is associated with reduced diamagnetic susceptibility (myelin loss) across cortical regions. [details](clinical/tatekawa-amyloid-chi-separation/summary.md)
- **Competing value signals impair reward-learning via dopaminergic mechanisms and increase exploration** (2026) — Reward learning selectively impaired by competing punishment signals; effect abolished by D2/3 antagonist amisulpride. [details](computation/2026-lin-competing-value-signals/summary.md)
- **Whisker stimulation reinforces a resting-state network in the barrel cortex: Nested oscillations and avalanches** (2026) — 11 Hz barrel circuit at rest is reinforced by whisker stimulation; slow oscillation phase gates avalanche occurrence. [details](computation/2026-07-25-whisker-stimulation-barrel-cortex.md)
- **MSBraM: A Multi-scale Self-supervised Brain Foundation Model for Hierarchical EEG Dynamics Learning** (2026) — Multi-scale tokenizer + curriculum masking pretrained on 2,400+ hours of EEG data; outperforms prior models across 10 downstream tasks. [details](computation/2026-07-23-msbram-eeg-foundation-model.md)
