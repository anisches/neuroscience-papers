# Neuroscience Papers

A growing collection of recent neuroscience papers, organized by topic.

## Recent Additions — July 30, 2026

- **Reading Without a Reader: Large Language Models Collapse Reading and Writing into a Single Entangled Code** (2026) — Saldaña Ulloa introduces an entanglement index E ∈ [0,1] (CKA, Procrustes residual, mutual k-NN) showing that decoder-only LLMs couple reading (WE) and writing (WU) codes at E=0.23–0.35, opposite to the brain's double dissociation; comprehension and production are positively coupled across all 12 non-degenerate models, placing LLMs as a distinct point in the space of possible minds. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-090000-saldana-ulloa-read-write-entanglement-lit)

- **Selective Impairment of Motor Recovery from Typing Errors in Parkinson's Disease: A Survival Analysis** (2026) — Bondade uses keystroke dynamics (backspace events from the neuroQWERTY dataset) with AFT survival analysis to dissociate error monitoring (spared in PD, r=0.164, p=0.413) from post-error motor recovery (strongly impaired with UPDRS severity, coefficient=0.0252, p=1.6×10⁻¹⁷), mirroring the known electrophysiological dissociation between preserved cortical ERN and impaired STN-linked post-error slowing. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-082000-bondade-typing-error-recovery-pd-lit)

- **More Electrodes, Faster Minds? Rethinking Bandwidth in Brain-Computer Interfaces** (2026) — Jiang provides a critical Perspective rebutting the "BCI I/O myth" — the direct scaling assumption that more electrodes proportionally accelerates thought output — distinguishing three narrowing levels (physical bandwidth, decodable information, subject-level confirmation) and grounding the bandwidth funnel in embodied cognition and the 10 bits/s constraint. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-083000-jiang-bci-bandwidth-lit)

- **When Branch-Local Shunting Helps: A Gain-Load-Alignment Principle for Dendritic E/I Networks** (2026) — Safaai, Richards, Khoshnevis & Sabatini introduce DendriNet, a trainable framework comparing divisive vs. additive E/I integration, establishing the gain-load-alignment principle: branch-local shunting helps when a reliable divisor suppresses more shared variability than it attenuates signal or introduces denominator noise; validated across three mouse V1 sessions showing the shunting-over-additive gap is largest for narrow readouts. [details](2026/safaai-shunting-gain-load-2026/summary.md) | [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-081322-safaai-gain-load-alignment-lit)

- **A Path Integral Model of Cognition** (2026) — Emori, Kondo, Iriki & Khrennikov develop a mathematical formalism for cognitive cost optimization via imaginary-time evolution on a projector Hamiltonian, with an exact discrete path-integral representation (no continuum limit) and a measurement-strength continuum interpolating between unconscious (weak coupling, interference-preserved) and conscious (strong coupling, projective collapse) processing. [zettel](note://cognition/2026-07-30-082600-emori-path-integral-cognition-lit)

- **CogEEGAgent: Toward Autonomous Cognitive EEG Analysis with Grounded Execution and Selection-Aware Verification** (2026) — Hou, Jiang, Lin & Yamada present an autonomous EEG analysis agent separating semantic authority (LLM routing) from scientific authority (deterministic code with trace-partition-evidence binding), including a Scientific Control Plane, Paradigm-Conditioned Verification (PCVR), and a held-out confirmation boundary achieving FPR 4.9% vs 16.5% best-p under adaptive search. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-084852-hou-cogeegagent-lit)

- **Decoding Error-Related Potentials under Multisensory Feedback with Varying Congruency** (2026) — Liu, Yin, Shin & Lee investigate how multisensory feedback (visual, auditory, tactile) with varying congruency affects ErrP decoding using a multi-branch EEGNet architecture with segmentation-and-reconstruction augmentation and auxiliary modality supervision, demonstrating robustness across heterogeneous sensory conditions. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-083000-liu-errp-multisensory-congruency-lit)

- **Mitigating Retention Loss in 65 nm Single-Poly Floating-Gate Analog In-Memory Computing** (2026) — Brazzini et al. demonstrate experimentally that the impact of retention loss on inference accuracy in floating-gate analog in-memory computing can be mitigated by combining Vread compensation (circuit-level) with batch normalization recalibration (algorithmic-level), recovering baseline accuracy within 2-4% after 60 days for VGG-10/CIFAR-10 and WRN-28-10/CIFAR-100. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-083000-brazzini-fg-retention-mitigation-lit)

- **Neuromorphic Masked Diffusion Language Models** (2026) — Wu, Ruah, Chen, Rajendran & Simeone introduce N-MDLMs, integrating block-parallel masked diffusion decoding with spike-based neuromorphic computation via IF neurons with bitwise temporal coding; spike sparsity (σ = probability of zero activation) reduces compute load, unlocking throughput on compute-bound in-chip memory systems where plain MDLMs show no improvement. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-082600-wu-nmdlm-neuromorphic-diffusion-lit)

- **Torque Fiber Proximal Dynamics: A Tuning-Free Variational Framework for Muscle Redundancy Resolution** (2026) — Ganji introduces TFPD, a tuning-free variational framework resolving muscle redundancy via Euclidean projection onto a time-varying convex polytope (torque fiber); it spontaneously generates triphasic-like EMG patterns and produces antagonist co-contraction as a geometric necessity of active-set transitions, not an imposed cost. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-ganji-tfpd-lit)

- **EEG Emotion Recognition From AI-Generated Biodigital Architecture Images** (2024/2026) — Yang & Guttmann-Flury use EEGNet to classify emotional responses (awe, disgust, content) to AI-generated biodigital architecture images from 52 participants, finding gamma band (30-45 Hz) best discriminates awe (77.07%), with SHAP attribution identifying greenery and non-uniform granularity as positive drivers. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-082001-yang-biodigital-eeg-lit)

- **Foundation Models for EEG Are Blind to Long-Range Temporal Correlations** (2026) — Zare probes five EEG foundation models (REVE, LaBraM, BENDR, CBraMod, BIOT) and finds none encode the DFA exponent of the alpha-band envelope, revealing a spectral-temporal dissociation where spectral-input models capture the static 1/f slope but not LRTC; the DFA exponent transfers directionally across populations (AUROC 0.575-0.740) while frozen FM embeddings do not beat chance. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-083000-zare-eeg-fm-lrtc-lit)

- **A Neural Network Model of Cultural Evolution** (2026) — Cox & Adams present a minimal ICA-based neural network model of cumulative cultural evolution where single-neuron agents learn by Hebbian plasticity to unmix hidden sources; a "Light" mechanism for selective social learning enables ratchet-like accumulation of harder-but-more-useful solutions, grounding inheritance (supervised learning) and variation (unsupervised learning) in synaptic plasticity. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-082000-cox-adams-cultural-evolution-lit)

- **GraphIDyOM: A Graph-Native Python Reimplementation of IDyOM for Musical Expectation Modelling** (2026) — Bono Rosselló reimplements IDyOM as GraphIDyOM, representing long-term and short-term predictive memories as explicit directed graphs; enables direct comparison of structural/compositional and predictive/listener-relative descriptions of music, with PPM-Decay bridging the model to age-dependent memory retrieval. [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-085500-bono-rossello-graphidyom-lit)

- **Forgetting Is Not a Fix: Path Dependence in Sequential Engram Editing** (2026) — Schessl tests the Compositional Memory States Hypothesis from the AI Engram literature, finding that sequential engram ablation is path-dependent (zero-shot vs. recalibrated editing diverge by 61-71%), overlap-ordered non-commutative (order decides whether uninvolved concepts survive), and subject to survivor drift (fatigue accumulation in every charge's own sufficient statistics). [zettel](note://neuroscience/2026-07-30-083100-schessl-engram-seq-lit)

## Recent Additions — July 28, 2026

- **The Anatomical Connections of Nucleus Accumbens with Brainstem and Cerebellum in the Rat and Human Subjects** (2026) — Cross-species mapping of NAc-brainstem/cerebellum connections using fluoro-gold tracing in rats and DTI tractography in humans, revealing conserved (raphe, VTA, parabrachial) and species-specific (dentate nucleus, locus coeruleus) pathways. [details](anatomy/cavdar-nac-brainstem-cerebellum-2026/summary.md)
- **Functional Deorphanization and Subtype-Selective Pharmacology of Three Tyramine Receptors in the Disease Vector, Aedes aegypti** (2026) — First functional characterization of three tyramine receptors (AaTAR1–AaTAR3) in Aedes aegypti mosquitoes, revealing TA-specific activation, high ligand specificity, and subtype-selective antagonist profiles, highlighting TARs as targets for next-generation insecticides. [details](2026/afifi-paluzzi-tyramine-receptors-aedes-2026/summary.md)
- **Transregional Neural Prostheses: Applications and Computational Tools** (2026) — Comprehensive review surveying transregional neural prostheses that re-establish endogenous causal communication within neural circuits, organized around a unified computational framework covering predictive models and stimulation optimization. [details](2026/wu-transregional-neural-prostheses-2026/summary.md)
- **Gp130 Orchestrates a Bidirectional Neuron-Microglia Circuit for neuroprotection** (2026) — Identifies gp130 as a master regulator of microglia-neuron communication; the LIF-IL6 positive feedback loop drives neuroprotection across TBI, stroke, and spinal cord injury models; therapeutic engagement via gp130-activating designer cytokines. [details](2026/willis-gp130-neuron-microglia-2026/summary.md)
- **Cross-Frequency Coupling as a Neural Substrate for Prediction Error Evaluation: A Laminar Neural Mass Modeling Approach** (2026) — Proposes that cross-frequency coupling (CFC) in laminar cortical circuits implements a hierarchical comparator mechanism for predictive coding via signal-envelope coupling (SEC) and envelope-envelope coupling (EEC); models disruptions in Alzheimer's disease and psychedelic states. [details](2026/ruffini-cfc-lanmm-prediction-error-2026/summary.md)
- **Active dendrites enable robust spiking computations despite timing jitter** (2026) — Burger, Rule & O'Leary propose a Leaky-Integrate-and-Hold (LIH) model showing that slow dendritic plateau potentials paradoxically enable fast, reliable spiking computation by acting as a resettable jitter buffer, maintaining 100% classification accuracy even when input jitter exceeds the membrane time constant by 10×. [details](computation/2026-07-28-burger-active-dendrites-robust-spiking.md)
- **Ramping-up hippocampal ripples and their neocortical coupling support human visual short-term memory** (2026) — Intracranial EEG reveals hippocampal ripples progressively ramp up during VSTM maintenance, predict successful performance, and couple with ripples in the lateral temporal lobe for memory reactivation, extending the role of ripples from long-term consolidation to short-term mnemonic processes. [details](human-brain/liu-hippocampal-ripples-vstm-2026/summary.md)
- **Individualized surface parcellation enhances characterization of resting-state brain dynamics and their alterations in schizophrenia** (2026) — Across two first-episode schizophrenia cohorts (n=414), individualized surface parcellation consistently outperforms volumetric approaches in detecting QPP dynamics and patient-control differences, but symptom associations remain difficult to replicate across cohorts. [details](computation/2026-07-28-watters-individualized-parcellation-schizophrenia.md)
- **Differential local synchronization in human nucleus accumbens vs. caudate and putamen** (2026) — Resting-state fMRI analysis of 1,055 HCP participants reveals NAcc has higher but steeper distance-dependent local synchronization than caudate/putamen, likely arising from differential neuromodulatory environments rather than cytoarchitecture. [details](human-brain/christova-georgopoulos-accumbens-synchronization-2026/summary.md)
- **Selective GPR17 antagonism enhances structural and functional recovery in animal models of demyelination** (2026) — The novel oral GPR17 antagonist RWT020016 promotes remyelination in acute and chronic cuprizone models (VEP latency recovery, Y-maze performance, MBP increase, g-ratio improvement), with human tissue validation showing GPR17 upregulation in demyelinated MS lesions. [details](anatomy/de-herdt-gpr17-antagonism-2026/summary.md)
- **Protective effects of Gamma-decanolactone against PTZ-induced behavioral alterations in Caenorhabditis elegans: behavioral and In Silico evidence** (2026) — Gamma-decanolactone dose-dependently reduces PTZ-induced paralysis in C. elegans via GABAergic signaling, with molecular docking confirming favorable binding to unc-25/unc-47/unc-49 targets; protection is absent in GABA-null mutants. [details](2026/mendes-gd-ptz-celegans-2026/summary.md)
