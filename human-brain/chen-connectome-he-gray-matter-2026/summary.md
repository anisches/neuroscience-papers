# Connectome-Based Mapping of Gray Matter Abnormalities in Hepatic Encephalopathy

**Authors**: Li Chen, Lei Xia, Yaling Chen, Chengkun Hong, Minghui Mao, Xiaoyang Wang, Lili Zhou
**Published**: 2026-07-10, *Frontiers in Medicine*, Vol 13, 1886303
**DOI**: 10.3389/fmed.2026.1886303
**PMID**: 42500531
**PMCID**: PMC13395610
**Tags**: `anatomy` `connectome` `hepatic-encephalopathy` `gray-matter` `disease-epicenter` `structural-connectivity`

## Summary

This study tested whether gray matter (GM) abnormalities in hepatic encephalopathy (HE) follow the organizing principles of large-scale brain networks. Using T1-weighted MRI from 45 HE patients and 45 healthy controls, cortical thickness (308 DK308 regions) and subcortical volumes (14 structures) were compared, and connectome-based analyses were performed using normative functional and structural connectomes from the HCP.

### Key Findings

1. **Widespread GM abnormalities** in prefrontal, motor, temporal, and limbic cortices, plus basal ganglia and thalamus
2. **No hub-preferential pattern** — abnormalities did not align with normative functional or structural hub regions
3. **SC-constrained spatial organization** — cortical abnormalities were positively correlated with structural neighborhood burden (r=0.58, Pspin=0.004), but not functional neighborhoods
4. **Functional-connectome epicenters**: left inferior frontal gyrus, orbitofrontal cortex, striatum
5. **Structural-connectome epicenters**: bilateral superior frontal gyri, left inferior frontal gyrus
6. **Individual-level heterogeneity** with partial prefrontal recurrence (highest SC hit rate 33.3%)

### Interpretation

HE-related GM abnormalities are non-randomly organized, constrained by structural connectivity architecture, and associated with prefrontal-centered disease epicenter networks. The absence of hub vulnerability suggests diffuse pathological processes (hyperammonemia, neuroinflammation, astrocytic dysfunction) rather than selective hub attack.

### Limitations

Cross-sectional design; modest sample size; normative (not patient-specific) connectomes; no cirrhotic non-HE control group.
