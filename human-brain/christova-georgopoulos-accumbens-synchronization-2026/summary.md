# Christova & Georgopoulos (2026) — Differential local synchronization in human nucleus accumbens vs. caudate and putamen

**DOI:** 10.1152/jn.00178.2026 | **PMID:** 42507821 | **Journal:** J Neurophysiol (ahead of print)

## Key Findings

1. **NAcc has higher but steeper local synchronization** than CN/Put — zero-lag crosscorrelation (CC0) between BOLD voxels is ~1.4× more distance-sensitive in nucleus accumbens.
2. **Power-law spatial decay with plateau at 8 mm** — CC0 strongest at 2 mm, decays by power law, flattens beyond 8 mm in all three nuclei.
3. **Caudate and putamen nearly identical** — their CC0 profiles are indistinguishable, consistent with shared functional roles.
4. **Attributed to neuromodulatory environment** — cytoarchitecture is comparable across nuclei, so differences likely arise from differential dopamine, serotonin, and acetylcholine receptor distributions (supported by human PET data).

## Methods

- **Data:** HCP resting-state BOLD fMRI, N = 1,055 participants
- **Analysis:** Zero-lag crosscorrelations between prewhitened time series from voxels 2–12 mm apart (six distance bins)
- **Fitting:** Power-law (log-log) fits to CC0-vs-distance curves per nucleus
