# State-Dependent Transcriptomic Collapse of the Brain's Lactate and Ketone Thermodynamic Sensors in Schizophrenia

**Date:** 2026-07-01  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.26.734782

Metabolic psychiatry has recently achieved unprecedented clinical rescue in treatment-resistant Schizophrenia (SCZ) utilizing targeted ketogenic interventions. This paper reports the discovery of a metabolic sensor array driving this pathology, identifying massive structural variances flanking the HCAR2/HCAR1 tandem locus—the brain's master thermodynamic governor. Structural collapse drives a transcriptomic double dissociation: downregulation of the HCAR1 lactate emergency brake and paralysis of the HCAR2 beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) and niacin cooling switch. This genomic etiology explains historical SCZ biomarkers and reframes Schizophrenia as an evolutionary "fuel mismatch" where the hominid brain experiences a thermodynamic crash when deprived of BHB by modern, high-glycemic diets.
